ℹ️ Platform Overview

HealthPulse AI — Enterprise Student Health Intelligence Platform

HealthPulse AI is an enterprise computational intelligence platform engineered to assess student physiological and lifestyle health risk levels. Utilizing Machine Learning classification alongside Groq LLM inference, the platform delivers instant risk scoring and clinical decision support.


Core Biometric Markers Evaluated:

  • Body Mass Index (BMI): Evaluates weight-to-height clinical ratios (Normal range: 18.5 – 24.9 kg/m²).
  • Sleep & Stress Ratios: Identifies sleep deprivation deficits and mental stress thresholds.
  • Resting Heart Rate: Tracks baseline cardiovascular indicators.
  • Daily Physical Activity & Hydration: Measures active exertion, step count, and daily fluid intake.
